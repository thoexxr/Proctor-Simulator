Q：抓学生却提示没有目标？

Technical Support

Updated: November 12, 2026

About the App

Proctor Simulator is a first-person game about catching cheaters in an exam room. You play as a proctor who patrols the room, observes students, and identifies cheaters while avoiding false accusations.

System Requirements

  • iPhone / iPad: iOS 15.0 or later, landscape orientation;
  • Mac: macOS 12 or later, supports Apple silicon and Intel.

Controls

Computer (Mac): WASD to move, mouse to look, left mouse button (or Space) to catch; press ESC during the game to open settings.

Phone / Tablet: use the lower-left virtual joystick to move, swipe the right side of the screen to look, and tap the lower-right button to catch.

FAQ

Q: The game says there is no target when I try to catch someone.
A: Aim the center crosshair at a student and stay within range. Catch after seeing a cheating action or alert to reduce mistakes.

Q: What if I wrongly accuse an innocent student?
A: False accusations reduce your reputation; when it reaches zero, the level fails. Confirm the student is cheating before accusing them.

Q: Achievements / leaderboards are not showing.
A: Make sure you are signed in to Game Center and have a network connection. Your best local score is shown on the in-game leaderboard.

Q: There is no sound.
A: Check your device volume and the mute switch in the in-app settings.

A：誤認は評価（レピュテーション）を減らし、ゼロになるとレベル失敗です。不正を確認してから指摘してください。
